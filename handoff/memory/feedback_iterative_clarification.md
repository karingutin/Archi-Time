When working on creative/conceptual design tasks (not pure coding), keep asking multiple rounds of concrete clarifying questions before producing a draft, even after several rounds already happened.

**Why:** during the [[architecture_of_time_question_bank_method]] design session on 2026-07-30, the user repeatedly asked to continue with more clarifying questions ("עוד שאלות", "שאלות מעולות מאמי תמשיכי לשאול" — "excellent questions, keep asking") across at least 4 separate rounds, even when many open decisions had already been resolved. She did not want to jump straight to a draft; she wanted the back-and-forth itself.

**How to apply:** for open-ended creative/design collaboration in this project, err on the side of one more targeted round of concrete multiple-choice clarifying questions (via AskUserQuestion) before proposing a synthesized draft, rather than assuming enough is known. Only move to drafting once she signals readiness (e.g. asks to see the draft, or stops requesting more questions).
