Answer in **Hebrew** and keep it **short**. No long structured reports, no tables unless
genuinely needed, no section headers for a two-line answer.

**Why:** she asked directly ("מותק תכתבי לי מתומצת ובעברית") after a run of long
English write-ups full of measurements and headings.

**How to apply:** lead with the answer. Facts and numbers only where they change her
decision. Keep verification detail out unless she asks or something actually failed —
still flag real failures and unverified claims honestly, just briefly.
Related: [[feedback-iterative-clarification]] — still ask clarifying questions before
creative work, but ask them short and in Hebrew too.
