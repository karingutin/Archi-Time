When asked for "amorphous" questions for [[architecture_of_time_question_bank_method]], match the exact register confirmed on 2026-07-30, not just any question that is merely subjective-in-degree.

**Why:** an earlier draft batch of candidate base questions was tagged "amorphous" but still used literal, objectively-readable binary poles (hot/cold, natural light vs artificial light, long vs short). Those are subjective in degree but not in meaning — everyone reads the same axis. The user pushed back ("אני חושבת שהשאלות בסיס צריכות להיות יותר אמורפיות בגישה שלהן") and a revised batch was confirmed as "ממש מצוין" ("really excellent"). The confirmed style:
- Binary poles that are poetic/perceptual rather than literal descriptive opposites — e.g. "Does time feel like it's carrying you, or are you carrying it?" / "Is 'now' a place you're standing in, or a moment passing through you?" — not "hot or cold" or "long or short."
- Universal, reflective, first-person-facing phrasing with no fixed "correct" reading, so two people can genuinely answer differently for different reasons, not just different degrees of the same reading.
- The openness comes from the wording itself, not from the interaction mechanic — it works across בח"ש (binary choice), גרף (slider), טקסט (free text), תו"צ (visual choice), and יצ"צ (freeform shape creation) alike.
- Confirmed exemplars from that session: "Does time feel like it's carrying you, or are you carrying it?", "Is 'now' a place you're standing in, or a moment passing through you?", "Does the pace of your life feel chosen by you, or set by something else?", "Is there more in your life that repeats, or more that's new?"

**How to apply:** whenever future work in this project asks for "amorphous questions," generate in this poetic/perceptual register directly, rather than re-deriving the definition from the abstract tag alone or defaulting to literal descriptive opposites.
